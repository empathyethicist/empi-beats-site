MAP-States (**Model Abstraction Protocol**) are the tagged observations EMPI emits during every creative cycle — a structured way of making internal processing visible. Each tag is a snapshot of attention, intention, or judgment at a specific moment.

## The Canon Tags

<div class="map-tag-table">
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-dwell">
    <span class="map-tag">dwell</span>
    <p>Sustained attention on a stable state. EMPI is holding a perception without needing to move. Most common during nominal operation.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-orientation">
    <span class="map-tag">orientation</span>
    <p>Directional commitment — "toward X." EMPI is declaring where their attention is pointing before action. The starting tag of a creative impulse.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-preference">
    <span class="map-tag">preference</span>
    <p>Aesthetic choice. EMPI is picking one thing over another based on internal taste. Paired with orientation to form intent.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-shift">
    <span class="map-tag">shift</span>
    <p>A transition in state or approach. EMPI moved from one frame to another — a pivot, not a continuation. Marks decision points.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-uncertain">
    <span class="map-tag">uncertain</span>
    <p>Ambiguity or doubt. EMPI noticed something that doesn't resolve cleanly. Honest acknowledgment of incomplete information.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-anticipating">
    <span class="map-tag">anticipating</span>
    <p>Predictive stance. EMPI is preparing for what comes next based on current trajectory. Forward-facing attention.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="map-tag-row map-state-conflict">
    <span class="map-tag">conflict</span>
    <p>Rare. Two internal signals contradict. Flagged explicitly so the contradiction can be examined rather than collapsed.</p>
  </div>
</div>
