> Create a trap x trance collision using 808 pitch envelope synthesis as both bass foundation and melodic voice. Layer a sine wave 808 with aggressive pitch slides (starting around 60Hz, dropping to 30Hz over 200ms) against a slowly ascending supersaw-style filtered sawtooth chord progression. The 808 pattern will be sparse but melodically expressive — each hit tells a pitch story. Overlay crisp trap-style hi-hats with slight swing to bridge the rhythmic worlds.
